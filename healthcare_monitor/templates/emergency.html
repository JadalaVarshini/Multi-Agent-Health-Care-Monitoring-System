Notifications are simulated in this demo (no SMS is sent). In a real emergency call your local emergency number (112 in India, 911 in the US).

Automatic triggers

  • Systolic ≥180 or <90; diastolic ≥120
  • Heart rate >130 or <40 bpm
  • Glucose >300 or <60 mg/dL
  • Temperature ≥39.5 °C or <35 °C
  • SpO₂ <90%
  • Red-flag symptoms (chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, …)
